# Apex Smile Dental Care - Landing Page

A modern, responsive, high-converting landing page built for **Apex Smile Dental Care**, a premium dental clinic specializing in anxiety-free, painless dentistry.

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Visual Design & Calming Theme**
   - Palette: Clean White (`#FFFFFF`), Soft Slate Gray (`#F8FAFC`), Deep Navy (`#0F172A`), Soothing Mint/Teal (`#0D9488`), and Warm Coral/Red (`#E11D48`) reserved exclusively for Emergency Call CTAs.
   - Typography: **Plus Jakarta Sans** with clean line-height and generous whitespace.
   - Glassmorphism accent cards and ambient radial blurs.

2. **Header & Floating Elements**
   - Sticky navbar with clinic branding, navigation links, and rapid contact actions.
   - Distinct **Dental Emergency** click-to-call button with phone icon and pulse indicator.
   - **Floating WhatsApp Button** fixed at the bottom-right with tooltip: *"Chat with Clinic / Send X-Ray"*.
   - **Mobile-First Sticky Bottom Action Bar** with instant buttons for *"Call Now"*, *"WhatsApp"*, and *"Book Slot"*.

3. **Hero Section**
   - Trust badge: `⭐ 4.9/5 on Google Reviews | 10,000+ Happy Smiles`.
   - Compelling headline and subheadline targeting dental anxiety relief.
   - Primary and secondary CTAs (*"Book an Appointment"* & *"Chat on WhatsApp"*).
   - Operatory image overlaid with floating glassmorphism badges (*100% Sterilized Instruments* and *Zero-Cost EMI Available*).

4. **Interactive Appointment Booking Bar**
   - Clean, 5-input booking form: Patient Name, Phone/WhatsApp, Treatment Needed dropdown, Preferred Shift dropdown (Morning / Evening OP), and Date picker (with auto-restricted minimum date).
   - Instant client-side validation and modal confirmation with reference ID (`APX-XXXX`) and direct WhatsApp reservation link.

5. **Core Treatments Grid**
   - 6 Interactive cards with Lucide icons, plain-language descriptions, and badges:
     1. Single-Sitting Root Canal (Painless rotary RCT)
     2. Dental Implants (Permanent natural-looking tooth replacement)
     3. Clear Aligners & Braces (Invisible teeth straightening)
     4. Laser Teeth Whitening (Brighter smile in 45 minutes)
     5. Wisdom Tooth Extraction (Safe, painless minor surgery)
     6. Pediatric Dentistry (Gentle dental care for kids)
   - *"Book Treatment"* button auto-selects the treatment in the booking form and smoothly scrolls to it.
   - *"Learn Details"* modal reveals procedure breakdown, anesthesia details, and estimated pricing.

6. **Trust, Hygiene & Technology**
   - **Interactive Before & After Image Comparison Slider**:
     - 60 FPS hardware-accelerated CSS `clip-path` with pointer capture (Mouse, Touch, Stylus).
     - Full keyboard accessibility (Arrow keys, Home, End).
     - Case study toggles: *Smile Whitening & Porcelain Veneers* and *Clear Aligners Straightening*.
   - **Painless & Safe Protocol**: 3 feature blocks highlighting 4-Step Autoclave Sterilization, Digital Low-Radiation Scanners, and Computer-Assisted Painless Anesthesia.

7. **Meet the Chief Dentist**
   - Profile of **Dr. Aryan Sharma, BDS, MDS (Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics)**.
   - Experience highlight (12+ Years, 6,000+ RCTs, DCI Reg. No: 18492-A).
   - Fellowships (ICOI USA, Certified Invisalign Provider, IDA).

8. **Payment & Transparency Banner**
   - Highlights 0% Interest EMI plans (Bajaj Finserv), UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Cards, and Insurance/TPA assistance.

9. **Google Patient Reviews & Clinic Location**
   - 3 authentic Google Reviews with verified tags: *"Painless RCT"*, *"No waiting time"*, and *"Very hygienic"*.
   - Live Clinic Open/Closed status indicator calculated from current system time.
   - Morning OP (9:30 AM – 2:00 PM), Evening OP (4:30 PM – 8:30 PM), Sunday (By Appointment Only).
   - Address, Landmark (*Opp. Metro Pillar 142, Above Starbucks*), Valet Parking info, and embedded Google Maps view.

10. **FAQ Accordion**
    - Smooth expand/collapse answers addressing dental anxiety, aligner costs, autoclave sterilization, and nocturnal emergencies.
